Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Placeholder for production legal review

This is a placeholder privacy policy for the ConnectNaija frontend prototype. It describes the intended approach for a production deployment. It is not legal advice.

Information We Collect

When you create an account or list a profile, we may collect: email address, phone number (optional), display name, age, location (state and city), biography, photos, relationship interests, and contact preferences.

How We Use Information

Information is used to operate the directory, display public profiles according to your privacy settings, process verification requests, moderate content, and communicate with you about your account.

Contact Information Visibility

You control whether your phone number or WhatsApp is shown on your public profile. We do not publish private contact details without your permission.

Verification Data

Identity documents submitted for verification are processed for the purpose of verification only. In production, such data should be handled securely and retained only as long as necessary for review.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share data with service providers who assist in operating the platform, or when required by law.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. You may pause or remove your listing at any time from your dashboard.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact support through the Contact page.

This prototype does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to a server. All interactions are client-side simulations.
