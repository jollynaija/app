Safety Guidelines

Last updated: October 2026

ConnectNaija is a directory for consenting adults. Your safety is important. Please read and follow these guidelines.

Before You Meet

  • Always meet in a public place for the first meeting — a café, restaurant, or busy mall.
  • Tell a trusted friend or family member where you are going and when you expect to return.
  • Do not share your home address or workplace until you know the person well.
  • Video call or speak on the phone before meeting in person when possible.
  • Trust your instincts. If something feels wrong, leave.

Protect Your Privacy

  • Use an alias or display name if you prefer not to use your real name on your listing.
  • Control whether your phone number or WhatsApp is visible on your profile.
  • Do not share financial information, bank details, or send money to anyone you meet through the platform.
  • Be cautious of anyone who pressures you for money, gifts, or personal documents.

Financial Safety

  • Never send money, airtime, or gift cards to someone you have not met in person and verified independently.
  • Be wary of stories involving emergencies, travel costs, or investment opportunities.
  • Arrangements should be mutually agreed and comfortable for both parties. Coercion is not acceptable.

Reporting

If you encounter a profile that is inappropriate, suspicious, or violates our terms, please report it. We review reports and take action where needed.

Age Policy

This platform is strictly for adults aged 18 and above. If you believe a member is underage, report the profile immediately. See our Age Policy for more details.

Emergency

If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. In Nigeria, dial 112 or your local police emergency number.
