Profile Verification

Build trust with a verified badge. This process is fully simulated — no documents or biometrics are uploaded or stored.

Why verify?

Verified members display a badge on their profile. Verification helps other adults feel more confident when connecting.

Requirements

  • A valid government-issued photo ID (National ID, International Passport, or Driver's Licence)
  • A clear selfie or liveness check matching the ID
  • You must be 18 years or older
Privacy notice: In production, identity documents would be processed securely and deleted after review. This prototype does not transmit, store, or process any real identity data.
