Age Policy

Last updated: October 2026

ConnectNaija is exclusively for consenting adults aged 18 years and above.

Strict Age Requirement

All users must be at least 18 years old. By creating an account, listing a profile, or using the platform, you confirm that you meet this requirement.

No Underage Content

We do not permit profiles, images, or communications involving anyone under 18. Any such content will be removed, and accounts may be terminated. We may report concerns to appropriate authorities where required by law.

Reporting

If you believe a member is under 18, please report the profile immediately using the "Suspected underage" reason.

Verification

Profile verification helps confirm identity and age. Verified members display a badge. Verification is recommended but not a substitute for your own caution.
